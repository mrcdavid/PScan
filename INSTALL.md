# Installing PScan (no coding needed)

PScan scans paper with your phone and saves the PDF to your PC, over your own Wi‑Fi. You
install two things: the **PC program** and the **phone app**.

## 1. On the PC (Windows 10 or 11)

1. Download **`PScan-Setup-<version>.exe`** (from the person who shared it, or the project's
   GitHub Releases page).
2. Double-click it.
   - Windows may show a blue **"Windows protected your PC"** box, because the app isn't from
     the Microsoft Store. Click **More info → Run anyway**. (PScan is safe; this warning
     appears for any app that isn't paid-signed.)
   - Click **Yes** when asked for permission to install.
3. Finish the wizard and leave **"Start PScan now"** ticked.
4. A small **PScan icon** appears near the clock (look under the **^** arrow). PScan now
   starts automatically each time you turn on the PC.

Finished PDFs are saved in a folder called **`PScan Scans`** inside
your user folder. To change it: right-click the PScan icon → **Change Scans folder…**.

Searchable PDFs work out of the box: the text-recognition engine is included.

## 2. On the phone (Android)

1. Copy **`PScan-<version>.apk`** to the phone and open it.
2. Allow **"Install unknown apps"** if asked, then tap **Install**.
   - If Play Protect warns, tap **More details → Install anyway**.
3. Open **PScan**.

## 3. Connect the phone to the PC (once)

1. Put the phone on the **same Wi‑Fi** as the PC.
2. In the app, tap the **PC** button at the top, then **Add another PC** (or just wait on the
   first screen). Tap your PC when it appears.
   - Not listed? On the PC, right-click the PScan icon to see its **IP address**, then type it
     in the app under "Or type the PC's address".
3. The PC shows a **6-digit code** (a notification, or right-click the icon → **Show pairing
   code**). Type it in the app.

Done. Tap **Add page**, photograph your document, and tap **Save PDF**. The PDF appears on the
PC and a notification pops up.

## Everyday use
- Keep the PC on with the PScan icon showing.
- Each person pairs their own phone with their own PC; the code keeps them separate.
- One phone can be paired with several PCs (home and office); it uses whichever one is on the
  Wi‑Fi.

## If the phone can't find the PC
- Make sure both are on the same Wi‑Fi and the PScan icon is showing on the PC.
- On an office/school network that blocks devices from talking to each other, turn on the
  **phone's hotspot** and connect the PC to it.
- Still stuck? On the PC, right-click the icon → **Open log folder** and send the log to
  whoever shared PScan with you.

## Uninstalling
Settings → Apps → **PScan** → Uninstall. Your pairings and saved PDFs are kept.

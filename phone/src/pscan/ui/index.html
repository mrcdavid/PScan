Why we built PScan

PScan was made for Marc so that documents no longer have to go through a printer scanner. Photograph each page with this phone and PScan sends it over the Wi‑Fi straight to Marc's PC. There the pages are straightened, cleaned up, made searchable and saved together as one A4 PDF, ready in the Scans folder.

How it works

  1. Snap each page with the phone camera.
  2. Send it privately over the local Wi‑Fi to the paired PC.
  3. Save: the PC builds a searchable PDF automatically.
